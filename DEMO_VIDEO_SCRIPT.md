# Demo Video Script

Target length: 2-3 minutes.

Speak naturally. You do not need to read every word exactly, but this script is designed to sound local and clear in English.

## 0:00-0:15 - Intro

Hi, I am introducing ThisOne, a fresh grocery assistant for a very common shopping moment:

You are standing in front of the shelf, looking at several packs that seem similar, and wondering:

Which pack should I actually buy?

## 0:15-0:35 - Problem

Fresh food labels often do not tell the whole story.

Two packs may both say beef rib, salmon, spinach, or apple, but the food inside can be different. One may be leaner, sweeter, crisper, fattier, more tender, fresher-looking, or better for a specific cooking style.

ThisOne is built for that decision.

## 0:35-1:20 - Product Demo

Here is the app.

First, I choose the shelf category. This prototype supports Fruit, Veg, Seafood, and Meat.

I can upload one shelf photo or one photo per package. For the demo, I will click Load demo scan.

Now ThisOne turns the options into A, B, and C.

In Step 2, it shows a compact comparison. For fruit, it compares variety, ripeness, sweetness, and best use. For meat, it compares part, fat, tenderness, freshness, and best use. Seafood also includes fat and tenderness. Veg focuses on part, freshness, texture, and best use.

The goal is not to show fake precision. It uses simple visible-cue estimates so the shopper can quickly understand the difference.

## 1:20-1:55 - Recommendation Flow

After the comparison, the user can stop, or ask ThisOne to help choose.

If I select Help me choose, the app asks a few simple questions:

When will I eat it?

How will I prepare it?

Do I have any dietary needs or preferences?

For example, I can type low sugar for fruit, or weight control and high protein for meat.

Then I click Pick this one.

ThisOne recommends one option and explains why in plain English.
